Figure 15. Unfiltered whole-trial angular-velocity spectra from all 72 frozen synthetic tracking trials. Each curve is the descriptive arithmetic mean of nine trial PSDs (three fixed path geometries × three reused noise seeds) for one method and plant condition; curves are not confidence intervals or nine IID route samples. Welch parameters: Fs=20 Hz, Hann window, 128 samples per segment, 64-sample overlap, 256-point FFT, constant detrending per segment, one-sided density scaling, and mean segment averaging. The dotted line marks 2 Hz. Whole continuous trials include startup and turning transients and have unequal durations; phases are never concatenated. Nonpositive PSD values are masked on the logarithmic axis without a pseudocount. The command clock is t−0.05 s and the body clock is t; this constant offset does not change magnitude PSD. Synthetic localization perturbations enter controller observations, not the saved true body-state trace directly. No FAST-LIO/NDT execution or real actuator identification is represented.
